# Weekly Session · surface brief

Scope: the guided Weekly Session flow at `/session`, full screen, phone first. Mode: Operate.
Audience and job: a woman running her own business, Sunday evening on her phone, turning quarter goals, a revenue target and rituals into one week she believes in, in twenty minutes.
Content: her real goals, revenue, rituals and calendar, entered in the session when missing; the Studio assistant drafts priorities and the week from that data.
Constraints: PRODUCT.md brand commitments (Pretty Focussed brand kit v1.0 is pinned and governs the whole app); English only; no chat; three priorities, five rituals, ninety days.
Unresolved: Google Calendar client id; Apple Calendar; whether the old guest pages stay reachable.

## Direction contract
